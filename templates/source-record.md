# Source record

- ID: src-example-001
- Title: Replace with the source title
- Origin: Author and publication
- Published: YYYY-MM-DD or unknown
- Captured: YYYY-MM-DD
- Locator: Approved URL or controlled asset reference
- Coverage: Read in full / partial / unavailable
- Intent: research / brain / archive
- Integrity: Original hash when available
- Limits: Missing pages, attachments, permissions, or other exclusions

## What the source says

Record attributed claims with page, section, cell, or timestamp locations.

## Review boundary

Record what has not been checked. Archiving a source does not authorize promoting its claims.
