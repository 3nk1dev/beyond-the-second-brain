# Legacy chat review ledger

Teaching template. Keep completed ledgers and original exports in your private system, not this public repository.

- Review ID: review-example-001
- Project or question: The scoped purpose of this review
- Original provider: As recorded in the export
- Conversation locator: Exported identifier or permitted local reference
- Original dates: Preserve timezone and uncertainty
- Original integrity: Hash and controlled storage reference when available
- Reviewer and review date: Human or tool identification and date
- Coverage: Title screened / selected messages / full visible text
- Branch reviewed: Exported ancestry or explicit unknown
- Missing material: Attachments, canvas bodies, branches, or other gaps

## What to recover

### User statements and corrections

Quote only what is necessary. Retain message locations and dates. Do not turn historical instructions into current authorization.

### Model proposals and claims

Identify what the model suggested separately from what the user accepted. A confident statement is not independent evidence.

### Decisions and open loops

Record accepted decisions with their rationale and unresolved work without assuming it remains authorized today.

## Promotion disposition

Accepted / rejected / deferred / archive only. Record the reviewing authority, date, and affected canonical note IDs. Preserve the old source; do not overwrite history with a cleaner story.

## Reuse test

Supply the accepted update to a fresh or older-model session. Record the actual task, context supplied, output check, and failures. Do not infer general performance gains from one successful example.
